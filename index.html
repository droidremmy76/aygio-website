01 / WHO WE ARE Possibility begins with people.
CONTINENTAL PURPOSE

Africa’s potential.
Young people at the centre.

The African Youth Growth & Innovation Organization connects youth development with the skills, confidence, and opportunities to lead transformative change.

From our headquarters in Monrovia, Liberia, our vision is a continent where every young African has the tools, mentorship, and agency to build thriving communities.

OUR MISSION

To provide accessible education, digital literacy, leadership development, vocational training, innovation platforms, and mentorship opportunities that help African youth realise their fullest potential.
